Water Wave

Our mission is to provide thrilling, safe, and unforgettable white water rafting experiences. We believe in the power of nature to connect people and build lasting memories.

History

Founded in 1993, our company started with a single raft and a passion for the river. Over the years, we have grown into a premier adventure outfitter, guiding thousands of happy guests down the rapids while maintaining our commitment to safety and environmental conservation.
